Design System & UI/UX Principles

The interface contract for the Pandora Knowledge Guardian. Every colour pairing carries a measured WCAG 2.1 contrast ratio; every principle states the reason it exists, not just the rule.

1 · Foundations

Surfaces only. No ramp value is ever used for text, which is what keeps the contrast table below predictable.

Colour — bioluminescence, one job each

Four accent hues, no more. A fifth would stop meaning anything: once a colour appears in two roles, the viewer must read the label anyway and the colour has become decoration.

2 · Principles

PRINCIPLE 01

Make the retrieval visible

The three-column layout puts knowledge base, question and retrieved sources on screen simultaneously. A pipeline stepper reports what actually ran: decompose → hybrid search → dedupe → rerank → ground → verify.

WhyThe product claim is "grounded, not guessed." A single chat bubble is indistinguishable from a plain chatbot, and the rules state a chatbot without retrieval does not qualify. Visible provenance is the interface's core job, not a debug affordance.
PRINCIPLE 02

Never encode state in colour alone

Every severity token pairs a distinct glyph, an explicit word and a colour. Legible in greyscale, to a colour-blind viewer, and through a washed-out projector.

▲ CRITICAL ◆ HIGH ■ MEDIUM ● LOW
WhyThis is not a generic accessibility nicety — the corpus mandates it. §11.1: "Do not use color alone for hazard levels; pair it with words and shapes." The knowledge base we are serving states the rule, so the interface obeys it.
PRINCIPLE 03

Uncertainty is a designed state, not an error

"Insufficient evidence" gets a full, composed screen: the withheld-answer badge, the closest retrieved chunk, its score against the threshold, and three recovery routes. It is styled in warn, never in alert — declining to answer is correct behaviour, not a fault.

WhyRequirement 6 demands explicit uncertainty handling, and 20 marks ride on grounded accuracy. A system that looks embarrassed when it refuses teaches the user to distrust the refusal — which is the one output most worth trusting.
PRINCIPLE 04

Separate documented protocol from inference

Two visually distinct regions. Protocol claims carry a record ID and a solid left rule; inference claims carry a dashed rule and the word inference. Evidence-quality chips label the grade of each source.

✓ Verified observation § Documented protocol ◇ Community tradition ~ Provisional interpretation ! Disputed report
Why§15.2 requires it: "For recommendations, separate documented protocol from model-generated inference." Chapter 1 defines the five evidence grades and asks that RAG systems preserve the labels, so they are metadata on every chunk rather than adjectives in prose.
PRINCIPLE 05

Bind every claim to a reachable source

Citation chips are baseline-aligned, 24px wide, with an invisible 44px hit target. Hover highlights the matching source card; click scrolls it into view and holds the highlight.

Restrict drinking and shellfish harvest, then sample at three depths 1. Fish movement offshore is a documented avoidance signal 2.

WhySuperscript chips at 10px violate Fitts's law and shift line-height. Baseline alignment with an oversized invisible target keeps the prose calm and the chip easy to hit — including on a trackpad, on stage, under pressure.
PRINCIPLE 06

Progressive disclosure over dense dumps

Answer first, then sources, then the raw chunk. Scores and match arms are collapsed until asked for. The knowledge panel shows corpus structure — 15 chapters, 188 chunks — not a wall of filenames.

WhyA judge has three to five minutes. Miller's limit is real: showing everything at once means nothing is read. The depth must exist for the sceptic and stay out of the way for everyone else.
PRINCIPLE 07

Respect the reading measure

Answer prose is capped at 68ch regardless of viewport. Excerpts clamp to three lines with a reveal.

✕ Unbounded

At 1400px the centre column runs past 110 characters per line, and the eye loses its place on every return sweep — the reader silently re-reads lines without noticing why the text feels tiring, which is exactly the wrong feeling to induce in someone evaluating whether your answer is trustworthy.

✓ 68ch measure

At 68 characters the return sweep is short enough that the eye tracks reliably. Comfortable prose reads as competent prose.

Why45–75 characters is the long-established readability band. The answer is the product; it has to be pleasant to read.
PRINCIPLE 08

Theme through restraint

Bioluminescence is carried by three cheap effects: two drifting radial light shafts, twelve rising spores, and glow confined to interactive and verified elements. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden and stops entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.

WhyThe original mockup ran 26 spores plus two 60vw blur(90px) gradients, compositing continuously. That is comfortable on a dev laptop and risky on unknown projector hardware. Dropped frames during the demo cost more than the extra particles ever earn.
PRINCIPLE 09

Keyboard and screen reader are first-class

Skip link is the first tab stop. All controls reach 44px minimum. The pipeline stepper is an aria-live="polite" region so progress is announced. Role and filter groups use real aria-pressed state. Focus ring is a 2px cyan outline with 2px offset, never removed.

WhyThe corpus asks for symbols and audio for people with limited literacy, and for confirmed understanding of emergency messages. A tool for emergency response that only works with a mouse and working eyes contradicts its own content.
PRINCIPLE 10

Every control must change something real

The Guardian / Researcher / Healer / Citizen switch applies an access-class filter to retrieval, per POL-005. A Citizen asking for clinical dosing gets a withheld-source notice, not a silently identical answer.

WhyA decorative control is worse than a missing one. A judge who clicks a persona, sees no change, and realises it is theatre will discount everything else on screen — including the parts that are real.
