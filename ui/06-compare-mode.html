The operational difference, in one lineContamination says restrict and sample. A vent plume says evacuate, then monitor gas. One investigates in place; the other moves people before measuring anything. That inversion is the answer to the question, and it is only visible because both records reached the context window.
Why this is a retrieval win, not a formatting trickThe aligned table is presentation. The substance is that two disjoint retrievals ran and were interleaved, so the volcanic half could not crowd out the contamination half. Before decomposition this exact question returned five volcanic chunks and zero INC-001 — it answered one side well and the other not at all. That is the failure the multiple-document-comparison use case is testing for.
Both sides withhold the same clinical detailAccess clearance applies per role, not per aspect, so a Guardian sees the same healer-limited notice on both columns. Consistency here matters: if clearance leaked on one side of a comparison, the comparison itself would be misleading.
