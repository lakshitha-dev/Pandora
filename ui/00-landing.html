The problem

Everything a guardian needs is written down. Nothing connects it.

Handling a yellow plume at Obsidian Reach means pulling together an incident playbook, a water-station reading, a severity scale, a species profile, a public alert policy and a sampling checklist — six records that never reference each other, sitting thirty pages apart.

Under time pressure nobody does that. And a general-purpose chatbot asked the same question will invent a confident, fluent, fabricated protocol. In an environmental emergency a plausible wrong answer is worse than no answer, because it gets acted on.

What it does

Three things a search box cannot.

1

Retrieves, then answers

Hybrid keyword and semantic search over the corpus, chunked on record boundaries so a species profile is never spliced into its neighbour. The answer is assembled from what came back — never from the model's own memory.

2

Cites every claim

Each sentence carries the record ID behind it — INC-001, WS-03, FAU-018 — with page, excerpt and relevance score. Click a citation and you land on the source.

3

Shows disagreement instead of resolving it

When records conflict, it presents every position side by side, names the reliability problem, and refuses to pick a winner. Naming a cause without evidence misdirects a response and assigns blame. That refusal is the feature.

How it works

"What caused the turquoise water at Awa Reef?"

◆ W2 ADVISORY ! Cause not established

INC-001 records the same two signs — water turning bright turquoise, fish moving offshore — and lists four possible causes: mineral sediment, plankton bloom, chemical release, light reflection. The record states plainly that these are hypotheses, not confirmed findings INC-001.

Three records disagree FN-A attributes it to a plankton bloom after three calm hot days. FN-B reports a damaged waste container at an upstream pigment workshop, but never confirmed material entered the water. LAB-C found harmless carbonates — with an incomplete chain-of-custody form, so it cannot break the tie. Recommended: resample upstream and downstream at matched depths with complete documentation, per checklist A.1 A.1.

Restrict drinking water and suspend shellfish harvest now, on precaution — not because a cause is known §4.5.
