What changed, and what did notAll three views cite the same eight records. Guardian gets imperatives and a do-not list. Researcher gets the measurement deltas, the evidence grades, and the methodology caveats — including the explicit note that the corpus does not link the oxygen fall to the bloom hypothesis. Citizen gets the corpus's own KC-01 communication template filled with retrieved evidence, so even the plain-language rendering is grounded rather than freely paraphrased.
Why re-render and never re-queryIf switching role re-ran retrieval, the cited evidence could shift between views and a judge would watch the facts change on screen — collapsing the grounding story the whole system is built on. The frozen-evidence strip above is the visible guarantee: those eight IDs are the input to all three renderings.
The one place this rule bendsAccess clearance is a different axis from register. A Citizen is not cleared for healer-limited clinical detail under POL-005, so clearance is resolved at query time from the active role and the toggle re-renders within it. Changing clearance is an explicit re-query, and the interface says so rather than silently returning a different evidence set.
