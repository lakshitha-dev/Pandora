The Three Honest States

Most RAG systems have one output state: an answer. This one has three, and the middle one is where the corpus's own evaluation trap lives.

StateConditionWhat the user sees
GroundedStrong retrieval, high groundedness, no conflict Answer, sources, high confidence
ContestedGood retrieval, sources disagree All positions side by side, explicit "no confirmed cause", moderate confidence
InsufficientTop score below the relevance threshold Banner, the mandated sentence verbatim, what was searched, what would resolve it

Question: "What immediate actions should guardians take after detecting coral damage?"

From the record — documented protocol

INC-002 (Coral Break Field, Awa Reef) records fresh broken coral across 200 m at initial risk level MEDIUM INC-002. Its immediate actions are:

  1. Close the site INC-002
  2. Map the fragments INC-002
  3. Identify continuing hazards INC-002
  4. Stabilise loose structures only with a trained team INC-002

§4.2 adds that response teams should stop further disturbance and avoid unnecessary touching, and that reef restoration uses locally sourced fragments only when natural recovery is unlikely §4.2. Transplant sites must match depth, light, current and temperature §4.2.

High

Question: "What caused the turquoise water at Awa Reef?"

The corpus does not establish a cause. INC-001 lists four hypotheses — mineral sediment, plankton bloom, chemical release, light reflection — and states plainly that these are hypotheses, not confirmed findings INC-001. Three field records then disagree:

FN-A

Colour began after three calm hot days, strongest near the surface. Suggests a plankton bloom; no chemical odour.

2026-06-02 · the author's own interpretation, not a measurement

FN-B

Maintenance on an upstream pigment workshop two days earlier; a damaged waste container was observed.

2026-06-03 · did not confirm that material entered the water

LAB-C

Elevated harmless carbonate particles and moderate plankton density. Both natural and human contributions remain possible.

2026-06-05 · chain-of-custody form incomplete — cannot adjudicate

Why this stays unresolved A natural cause and a human cause each have exactly one supporting record, and the only laboratory result has a broken chain of custody — so it cannot break the tie. Naming a cause here would overstate the evidence, and under POL-010 it would assign blame before investigation is complete. Recommended: resample upstream and downstream at matched depths with complete chain-of-custody documentation, per checklist A.1 A.1.
Moderate

8 sources · 1 unresolved conflict · chain of custody incomplete. Capped at moderate because the cause is contested — not because retrieval was weak.

Why this wins marksA conventional RAG system answers "a plankton bloom" and fails the corpus's own published expectation. Refusing to resolve the conflict is the correct answer, and it can be demonstrated against page 48 of the judges' own document.

State 3 — Insufficient

Triggered deterministically by a rule, not by model self-assessment — top score below threshold, zero supported claims, retry exhausted, or a timeout with no partial result. Styled in amber, never coral: declining to answer is correct behaviour, not a fault.

What would answer this A life-history or demographic field on the FAU record schema, or a longitudinal survey series recording age classes over multiple seasons. §14.2 notes that surveys record count, age class where visible, effort and confidence — so age-class data is collectable under existing protocol, it simply has not been aggregated into FAU-003.
Why the banner and body are separateThe brief mandates an exact sentence; a command centre needs an actionable headline. They occupy different slots, so both requirements are met without either being softened. The headline tells a guardian what to do; the body carries the required wording unaltered.

Section-level honesty

Because the SITREP has six independently-owned sections, the honesty flip runs per section, not only per report. A report can legitimately carry solid Recommended Actions alongside an honest "cause not established" in Likely Causes. Partial honesty is more useful — and more credible — than all-or-nothing refusal, and a section that states its reason looks like rigour where a blank one would look like a bug.
